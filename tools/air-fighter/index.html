Air Fighter

A 90s-arcade vertical shoot-'em-up. Pick one of three fighters, blast through 5 stages and their bosses, grab power-ups, drop screen-clearing bombs — with chiptune music and sound, fully offline.

📻 HQ RADIO

How to use

  1. Desktop: ←↑→↓/WASD move · Z/Space fire · X/Shift bomb · P/Esc pause · F autofire · M mute.
  2. Mobile: drag anywhere to fly (autofire is on), tap the red button to bomb.
  3. Grab P to power your shot up to level 5, B for extra bombs, ⭐ medals for score, 1UP for lives.
  4. Each stage ends with a boss — watch its health bar and dodge the patterns. Bombs also erase enemy bullets.
  5. Your fighter choice, hi-score and sound settings are remembered on this device.
  6. Hangar rumor: HQ still listens on an old radio channel — the wavy key beside 1 opens it (pilots on touch screens tap their score three times). They say HQ sends exactly what you ask for… but supply drops never make the record books.
