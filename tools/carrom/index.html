Carrom

Flick the striker, pot your coins and cover the Queen before the bot does. A physics-based board with slingshot aiming, three AI levels and four hand-built themes — all offline, on your device.

White always breaks first.

How to play

  1. Position the striker: drag it left/right along your baseline (the line nearest you).
  2. Aim & shoot: press anywhere else on the board and pull back — the striker is a slingshot. The white guide shows where it will go; the further you pull, the harder the flick. Release to strike.
  3. Pot your colour to score and keep your turn. Miss, or foul, and it’s the bot’s turn.
  4. The Queen (red) must be covered: pot one of your own coins on the same or next stroke, or she goes back to the centre. A covered Queen is worth 3.
  5. Pocket the striker, hit nothing, or pot the bot’s coin = foul (a coin comes back as penalty and your turn ends).
  6. Clear all your coins to win the board. Board score = bot’s coins left (+3 for a covered Queen). First to the target points wins the match.

Physics-based and fully offline. Rules follow standard carrom with a couple of pragmatic simplifications (e.g. you may finish while the Queen is still on the board — she just scores for nobody). Your settings are remembered on this device.
